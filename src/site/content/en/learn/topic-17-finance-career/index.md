## Why this track

Technical skill grows your income; financial skill keeps and multiplies it. Most engineers
earn well but never design their money. This track turns that around: clear, beginner-friendly
lessons on managing, protecting, and growing what you earn — written for an engineer's mindset.

## How it works · Cách học

8 lessons, ~20 minutes each. Read the English first, then the Vietnamese right below to confirm
understanding. Each lesson ends with one concrete action to take this week.

8 bài học, mỗi bài ~20 phút. Đọc tiếng Anh trước, rồi đọc tiếng Việt ngay dưới để chắc chắn hiểu
đúng. Mỗi bài kết thúc bằng một hành động cụ thể để làm trong tuần này.

## Lessons · Các bài

1. Your money map — income, spending, savings rate
2. The emergency fund — your financial safety net
3. Understanding compensation — base, bonus, equity, remote pay
4. Negotiating salary and raises
5. Freelance & remote income — contracts, invoicing, tax basics
6. Budgeting your AI learning — free, cheap, and paid resources
7. Investing basics — index funds, risk, and time
8. Building wealth as a senior engineer — multiple income streams
