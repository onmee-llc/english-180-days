## Session goal

Understand the simplest, evidence-based way to grow money over the long term.

## Core ideas

- **Invest only after** your emergency fund is full and high-interest debt is gone.
- **Compounding** is exponential — the earliest money you invest does the most work. Time beats timing.
- **Low-cost, broad index funds** (e.g. global or S&P 500 ETFs) beat most active strategies over decades.
- **Risk = volatility you can tolerate.** Match it to your time horizon: long horizon → more equities.
- **Automate and ignore.** Regular fixed contributions (dollar-cost averaging) remove emotion.
- Beware anything promising fast, guaranteed returns — that is how people lose money.

- Cảnh giác mọi thứ hứa lợi nhuận nhanh, được đảm bảo — đó là cách người ta mất tiền.
