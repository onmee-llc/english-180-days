# Topic 19 — AI/ML Security & LLM Red Teaming

**Target Audience:** Senior Backend & AI Engineers, AI Architects, Technical Leads.  
**Focus:** Explaining security architectures, threat vectors, defense-in-depth mechanisms, and risk trade-offs in modern AI systems with precision and authority in English.

---

## 🎯 Program Goals

By the end of this 14-day track, you will be able to:
1. **Threat Model AI Systems:** Confidently break down the attack surface of LLM applications, training pipelines, and multi-agent systems in English.
2. **Defend Against Prompt Injection:** Articulate direct and indirect prompt injection vectors, delimiter escaping, and multi-layered guardrails to technical interviewers.
3. **Navigate Compliance & Frameworks:** Discuss NIST AI RMF, OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications, and EU AI Act requirements with senior stakeholders.
4. **Conduct Red Teaming:** Explain automated fuzzing, jailbreak evaluation harnesses, and incident post-mortem methodologies.
